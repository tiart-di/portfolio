Context

How it started

SEO Article Generator began as a blog inside iAsk and grew into its own B2B product. It started as a finished idea with no design.

Goal

An article users can copy, paste and publish, without the AI slop.

The Problem

Users didn't trust AI-written articles

Problem

AI SEO writers often produce text full of AI slop and unverified facts. Users still edit heavily, re-read everything and often have to hunt for sources.

I ran 12 Zoom interviews with content marketers. The biggest problem was text that sounds like AI. They didn't trust it, so every article needed a full read-through and a source check.

01

11 of 12 re-read every article in full before publishing.

02

7 of 12 looked for sources on their own.

03

40 minutes to 1.5 hours went into getting one article ready.

Finding

Koala links to sources in the text, but none of the four checks text with an AI detector. No tool combines humanizing, AI detection and sources in one flow.

Where I chose to be simpler

Frase is stronger for professionals: a full editor with scoring, checklists and keyword research. This product goes the other way. It is closer to SeoWriting.ai: a fast, simple flow for small teams that need an article, not a content suite.

The Solution

How the tool works

Solution

Write it personalized, humanize it, check it with an AI detector and link the sources. The goal is an article the user can copy and paste.

01

Keyword. Country, language, tone and audience shape the writing. The analysis is free.

02

Brief. Pick a title, edit the outline, and add topics that show how many of the top 10 pages cover them.

03

Editor. The article arrives humanized, with an AI-detection score and numbered claims linked to sources.

Decisions

Key design decisions

01

A brief before writing. The tool doesn't generate right away. The user first sees a brief with title options, an editable outline and the topics that top-10 pages cover. It gives users more control, and with it more trust in the result.

02

Humanizing is built into writing. We discussed a separate humanizer tool, but built it into article creation. The user sees one loading step, “Humanizing your article”, and then a finished text. No extra screens or settings: the problem was solved in the prompt, not in the interface.

03

No article below 85 reaches the user. The article is released only when its AI-detection score is 85 or higher, so users never see a weak result. The idea: a guarantee builds more trust than a number the user has to interpret. The score panel in the editor confirms it.

04

Free analysis, credits per article. Keyword analysis costs nothing, and credits are spent only when an article is generated. Users see the value first and pay once they know what they will get.
